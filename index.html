Music Theory · Section I style · 5 questions · about 2 minutes

Cadence Catcher

Listen to a four-bar chorale phrase and name the cadence that ends it.
Directions: Each question refers to a four-voice excerpt. Play the excerpt as many times as you like, then select the best answer. Revealing the score lowers the points available.

About this demo

  • Original stimuli. Five four-voice phrases, voice-led without parallel fifths or octaves. Three share the same opening so the cadence is the only thing that changes.
  • Ear first. Answering without the score earns 2 points, which nudges players toward aural identification; revealing the score drops it to 1.
  • Feedback that teaches. Every answer explains the harmony, gives a "listen for" cue, and offers a retry round for missed questions.
  • Documented like a real item. Each question includes item notes: the skill assessed, expected difficulty, and the reasoning behind its strongest distractor.
  • Accessible by design. Full keyboard control, visible focus, screen-reader announcements, and alt text for every score that describes pitches without giving away the answer.
